Wisp Forest

Luminous platform adventure

Ori-lite vertical slice trong mini game library.

Chay, nhay, wall-slide, dash va ban spirit bolt de mo duong den den thieng cuoi rung.

Wisps 0 / 5
Health 4
Checkpoint Awakening Pool

Controls

  • A / D hoặc Left / Right: move
  • Space / W / Up: jump và double jump
  • Shift / K: dash
  • J / Z: spirit bolt
  • R: reset run

Goal

Thu thap 5 wisps, mo Forest Shrine va vuot qua doi patrol spirits de cham den den thieng.

Design

Ban clone nay giu cam giac platformer cinematic va movement mượt, nhung scope du nho de song chung voi cac mini game khac trong repo.
