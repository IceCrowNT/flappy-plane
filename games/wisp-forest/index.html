Wisp Forest

Awaken the forest

Collect wisps, manage energy, survive spirit patrols, then break the shrine sentinel in a final arena.

Controls

  • A / D or Left / Right: move
  • Space / W / Up: jump and double jump
  • Shift / K: dash
  • J / Z: spirit bolt
  • P / Esc: pause
  • R: restart run

Goal

Collect 6 wisps, open the Shrine Gate, then defeat the Shrine Sentinel before climbing to the sanctum.

Design

This upgrade is denser than the first slice: energy economy, ranged enemies, pause state, and a proper boss finale instead of a simple finish trigger.
